Basis of Calculation

AR Eligibility (Regular): Received (Net) + Total Discount ≥ Due Amount.

AR Eligibility (Imported): Due > 0 — imported entries skip the received/discount rule.

AR Monthly: 1% of (Amount Received + Total Discount), rounded to nearest rupee. Can be manually overridden.

Split Schedule (Multiple Payees): Click "Split Schedule" in the cheque section to divide the monthly AR between 2 or more parties (e.g. A & B). Each payee gets their own share % and their own cheque lot. Auto Generate will create one lot per payee.

TDS: 10% of the monthly AR amount.

Total Payable: Net Monthly AR × Number of issued cheques (imported) or AR Period.

Unit No. Uniqueness: Every entry needs a Unit No. If it already exists for another client, you'll be asked for a reason.

Auto Generate (Safe Mode): Auto Generate will never modify or overwrite existing lots. If a schedule already exists for an entry, it is skipped. Use New / Update Lot to add/modify manually, or the Delete button on the lot header to remove a lot.

Multiple Delete: In the Entries tab, tick checkboxes and click "Delete Selected".

Full Import: Excel import captures EVERY row detail — all payments (wide format: Payment 1, 2, 3...N), all discounts, PAN, Favouring, Control No, Company, Unit No., AR Period, Due Amount, and all multi-sheet data.

Schedule Sheet Import: If Excel has a separate sheet with cheque schedule (with "Source Sheet" + "Cheque Date" columns), those are auto-linked to matching entries.

Merge by Description: In "Merge by Client" mode, rows merge by Description (Unit No.), not by client name — since same client can have multiple units.

AR Increase / Top-Up Lot (Accumulated Mode): If a new payment comes in after cheques are already issued, the tool detects the increase and lets you create a separate Top-Up Lot. The 1st cheque of Top-Up Lot carries the ACCUMULATED DELTA for the entire period from the payment date up to 1 day before the next cheque date (i.e., original lot's last cheque date + 1 month − 1 day). Subsequent cheques carry the full monthly delta. Original lot stays completely untouched.
